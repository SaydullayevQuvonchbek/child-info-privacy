Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CHILD-INFO ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information from users of our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Our app only collects the following minimal information:

  • Name: Users voluntarily enter only their first name to personalize their experience. No email, phone number, or password is required.
  • App usage data: We may collect anonymous data about how users interact with games and tasks within the app (e.g., completed tasks, game scores) to improve the app experience.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Email addresses or passwords
  • Phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Photos, videos, or microphone input
  • Device contacts
  • Payment information

3. Children's Privacy

CHILD-INFO is designed for children. We are committed to protecting children's privacy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the first name entered voluntarily. We do not share children's data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. How We Use Information

The name entered by the user is used solely to personalize the in-app experience (e.g., displaying "Hello, [Name]!"). It is not stored on external servers or shared with third parties.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties. We may use standard analytics tools (such as Firebase Analytics by Google) to understand general app usage patterns. These tools collect only anonymized, aggregated data.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information within our app. Since we collect minimal data, the risk to user privacy is very low.

7. Data Retention

The name entered by the user is stored only on the user's device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app. We do not retain personal data on our servers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
